अनन्य चेत

ANANYA CETA

"An undivided mind, forever turned toward Krishna" — where ancient devotion meets modern code.

Ananya means unwavering, exclusive, without another — and Ceta means consciousness, the seat of awareness. Together, the name carries a single vow: a mind that does not scatter, held always in remembrance of Krishna. We are a community of builders, writers, and seekers who believe technology, too, can be an offering — a way to carry dharma into the world people actually live in today.

THE VERSE WE STAND ON

Ananya Bhakti, Explained

अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते ।
तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम् ॥

NEPALI

जो भक्तहरू अनन्य भई सधैं मेरो चिन्तन गर्दै मेरो उपासना गर्छन्, त्यस्ता नित्य युक्त भक्तहरूको योगक्षेमको भार म आफैं वहन गर्छु।

ENGLISH

To those who worship Me with an undivided (ananya) mind, ever absorbed in Me, I carry what they lack and preserve what they have. — Bhagavad Gita 9.22

This single verse is where our name is born. Ananya Ceta exists to hold that same undivided attention in a distracted age — publishing shlokas beside their Nepali and English meaning, building tools that make study easier, and gathering a community around one unwavering center.
